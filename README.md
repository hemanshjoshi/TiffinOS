# TiffinOS — Claude-Powered Ops for Cloud Kitchens

## Overview
TiffinOS is an open-source, Claude-powered operations layer designed for small food businesses that run on WhatsApp and manual workflows.

It provides structured systems, prompt libraries, and operational templates to help tiffin services and cloud kitchens run efficiently without complex software.

---

## Problem
Most small food businesses operate using:
- WhatsApp chats
- Manual tracking
- Memory-based workflows

This leads to:
- Missed or incorrect orders  
- Untracked tiffin inventory losses  
- Inconsistent customer communication  
- Low retention and repeat rates  

---

## Solution
TiffinOS introduces a lightweight AI-assisted operations system powered by Claude, enabling:

- Automated customer communication flows  
- Structured order confirmation systems  
- Tiffin tracking and accountability  
- Retention and engagement strategies  
- Daily operations optimization  

---

## Key Modules

### 📲 WhatsApp Automation
- Menu distribution workflows  
- Confirmation & no-response logic  
- Delivery and delay communication  

### 🍱 Tiffin Tracking
- ID-based container tracking system  
- Return management workflows  
- Loss reduction strategies  

### 🔁 Customer Retention
- Sunday special strategies  
- Feedback loops  
- Win-back campaigns  

### 🧠 Claude Prompt Library
- Operations management prompts  
- Delivery optimization  
- Customer behavior analysis  
- Business growth insights  

---

## Real-World Context
This project is inspired by real operational challenges faced while running a tiffin service (Maakhana, Jaipur).

The systems and workflows are based on practical, day-to-day execution problems such as:
- Handling bulk meal confirmations  
- Managing reusable tiffin inventory  
- Coordinating deliveries within tight timelines  
- Retaining customers in a competitive environment  

---

## Why Claude
Claude acts as an intelligent operations assistant by:
- Reducing manual decision-making  
- Generating structured outputs  
- Supporting real-time operational workflows  

This makes advanced operational systems accessible even to small, non-technical businesses.

---

## Ecosystem Impact
Small food businesses form a massive but underserved segment globally.

TiffinOS aims to:
- Standardize operations for tiffin services and cloud kitchens  
- Reduce inefficiencies in low-tech business environments  
- Enable adoption of AI in everyday business workflows  

---

## Vision
To become the default operating system for small food businesses, starting with tiffin services and expanding to PGs, hostels, and micro-restaurants.
